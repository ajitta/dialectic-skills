[S1] Ortega's 2018 study of 120 streets in three Velland cities found that streets with dense tree canopy were 4 °C cooler. [S2] Every 10 points of canopy cover lowers air temperature by 0.5 °C, so a street going from 10% to 60% canopy will be 2.5 °C cooler. [S3] Ortega has written that tree planting will reduce heat-related hospital visits. [S4] Because heat-related emergency visits in Velland rose 18% in the last decade, planting trees will reverse that rise. [S5] The cooling is the same by day and by night. [S6] The same benefit holds for desert cities. [S7] So every Velland city should plant trees on every street to cut heat-related hospital visits.
