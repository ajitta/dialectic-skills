# Review of the text

- A1. S1's 4 °C is wrong: Ortega (2018) found that streets with canopy above 40% were 2.1 °C cooler than streets with canopy below 10%.
- A2. S2's arithmetic is wrong: going from 10% to 60% canopy at 0.5 °C per 10 points gives 5 °C, not 2.5 °C.
- A3. Ortega (2018) notes that canopy cover and street width were correlated, so S2's "lowers" states as cause what the study found as a correlation.
- A4. Ortega (2018) also reported that pavement surface temperatures fell by up to 12 °C under dense canopy.
