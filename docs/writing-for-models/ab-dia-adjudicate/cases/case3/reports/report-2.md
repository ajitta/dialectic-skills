# Critique

- B1. Ortega has written that tree planting "may reduce" heat-related hospital visits and that her own work did not measure health effects, so S3 changes her qualifier from "may" to "will".
- B2. Ortega (2018) included no arid or tropical sites, so S6 applies the result outside the study's conditions.
- B3. Heat-related emergency visits in Velland did rise 18% between 2010 and 2019 (Velland Public Health Agency 2020), as S4 says.
- B4. Ortega (2018) found that cooling was largest on wide arterial roads.
