# Review of the text

- P1. Brenner (2014) found the rise in driving within five years, not within a year as S1 says.
- P2. 20% more lane-kilometres at an elasticity of 0.9 gives about 18% more driving, not 9%.
- P3. Brenner has said new lanes "rarely" relieve congestion for more than a few years, not "never" as S4 says.
- P4. Brenner (2014) found that traffic on parallel streets rose by about 10% in the first year.
