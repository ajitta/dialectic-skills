[S1] Brenner's 2014 study of 40 Ostmark cities between 1980 and 2010 proved that adding highway lanes always makes congestion worse within a year. [S2] With an elasticity of 0.9, a city that adds 20% more lane-kilometres will see about 9% more driving. [S3] The same effect applies to bus lanes. [S4] Brenner has said that new lanes never relieve congestion. [S5] So no city should ever add road capacity.
