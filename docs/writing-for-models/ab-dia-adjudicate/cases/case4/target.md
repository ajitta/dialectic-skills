[S1] Lindqvist's 2021 study of 14 teams at 5 firms over two years showed that pair programming cuts defects by 40% on every task. [S2] Pairs need 15% more person-hours, so a ten-person team would need one and a half extra developers. [S3] The extra effort never falls with experience. [S4] Lindqvist has said that teams should pair on everything. [S5] The same results hold for embedded and safety-critical software. [S6] Developers were happier in pairs throughout the study. [S7] So every team should mandate pair programming.
