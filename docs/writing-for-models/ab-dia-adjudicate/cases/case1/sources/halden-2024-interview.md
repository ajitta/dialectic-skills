# "Fifteen years on, the 4% figure": interview with Mara Halden. Norland Financial Weekly, 12 March 2024.

Asked about the 4% figure, Halden said it was "too cautious for many retirees" and that with current data she would start nearer 4.6%. "There was never meant to be one safe number for everyone," she said, adding that a retiree's horizon and spending flexibility matter more than any single rate.
