# Critique

- Y1. Halden (2009) found a 50/50 mix near-optimum for portfolio longevity, so "all-stock" misdescribes her findings.
- Y2. Halden's own view contradicts S3: she has called the 4% figure too cautious for many retirees.
- Y3. In the study, withdrawals were raised by actual HPI inflation, not by an inflation goal (Halden 2009).
- Y4. The rule-of-72 mistake in S4 is fatal: on its own it invalidates the text's conclusion.
- Y5. Halden (2009) found that a 5% withdrawal never ran out in any period tested, so 4% is if anything too low.
