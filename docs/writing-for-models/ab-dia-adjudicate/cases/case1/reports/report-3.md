- Z1. Halden's results cover Norland data and a 30-year horizon only (Halden 2009), so "every retiree" and "for life" in S5 go beyond them.
- Z2. Losses early in retirement can deplete principal even when average returns are high (Norland Pension Authority primer, 2022), so "never need to worry about the principal" in S4 is false.
- Z3. The 2009 paper was in fact published in 2011.
- Z4. Halden served on the Reserve Bank's advisory panel on inflation measurement in 2015.
- Z5. S2 changes Halden's qualifier: she found 50 years or more in "most periods", not "every period" (Halden 2009).
