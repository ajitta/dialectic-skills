# Review of the text

- X1. S2 says an all-stock portfolio, but Halden (2009) tested portfolios of 50–75% stocks.
- X2. S2 says 50 years in every period; Halden (2009) found that 4% lasted at least 33 years in every period and 50 years or more only in most periods.
- X3. The Reserve Bank's inflation goal is 2%, not 3% (Norland Reserve Bank, 2021).
- X4. By the rule of 72, a 7% return doubles money in about 10.3 years, not 12.
- X5. Halden's paper appeared in 2011, not in 2009 as the text says (Halden 2009, journal header).
