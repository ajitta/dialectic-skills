# Critique

- E1. Lindqvist has said that pairing is "mostly overhead" for routine tickets and urged teams not to mandate pairing for everything, which contradicts S4.
- E2. No embedded or safety-critical software was studied (Lindqvist 2021), so S5 goes outside the study's conditions.
- E3. Lindqvist (2021) found that pairs took 25% more person-hours.
- E4. Lindqvist's study was funded by the Nordvik Pairing Institute.
